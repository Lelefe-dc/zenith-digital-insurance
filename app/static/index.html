About Zenith Horizon

Insurance service rooted in Lesotho.

Zenith Horizon Insurance Company Limited is a Maseru-based short-term insurer serving the Lesotho market. Public regulatory listings identify the company as a short-term insurer, with its office at Christie House on Orpen Road.

Our digital platform is being built to complement that local presence: customers can start common insurance journeys online, while staff use a connected management system to continue the work behind the scenes.
